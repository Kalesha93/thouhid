Our History

Journey Of Excellence

Established in 2001, GlobalTech University has grown into one of the leading
institutions offering quality education across engineering, management,
sciences and technology.

With modern infrastructure, experienced faculty and strong industry
partnerships, the university continues to prepare students for global careers.

To become a globally recognized institution fostering innovation,
research and leadership.
